# Interlayer Exchange Coupling

We want to learn about the oscillatory couplings between transition metals through non magnetic noble metals as spacer materials. This 
oscillatory behavior is simlar to the RKKY interactions between magnetic impurities. However for a simple model such

- Free electron model with continous spin distribution within ferromagnetic layers

the RKKY theory predictions differ from experiments. The RKKY theory does not describe correctly the magntitude and phase of the coupling oscillations because it doesnt describe correctly the interactions between the ferromagnetic layers and the conduction electrons.

## RKKY Theory

### Model

Consider a two ferromagnetic monolayers $F_1$ and $F_2$ separated by a non-magnetic spacer of thickness $z=(N+1)d$ where $N$ is the number of atomic planes and $d$ the spacing between each plane. Experimentally, the thickness of the magnetic layers dont influence the coupling, then as a first approximation we only consider one atomic layer for each ferromagnet  layer.
